ICPC Guidelines

Important information and rules for the ICPC competition

General Guidelines

  • Teams must consist of exactly three members.
  • All team members must be currently enrolled students at BUBT.
  • Team names must start with "BUBT_" followed by your team name.
  • Each student can only participate in one team.
  • Team registration requires valid student IDs and ICPC global email addresses.
  • Once confirmed, team names cannot be changed.
  • Teams must provide a valid phone number for communication.

Important Notes

Make sure all information provided during registration is accurate. Incorrect information may lead to disqualification.

If you need to make changes to your team after registration, use the "Edit Team" option with the phone number you provided during registration.

For any questions or issues, contact the competition organizers.
